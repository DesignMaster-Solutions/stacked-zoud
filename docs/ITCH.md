## Short description

Sale season in Dubai. Every "pay in 4" lands on a payday you have not lived yet. Watch the stack before it falls.

## Description

<p><strong>Stacked</strong> is a financial literacy game made for Zoud GameLab (theme: Make FinLit Fun).</p>
<p>It is sale season in Dubai. Offers arrive three a month: sneakers, a phone, a weekend trip, and also the things you need, like a car repair, the fridge that died, school fees. Each offer has three buttons: pay now, split in 4, or skip.</p>
<p>Splitting feels free: only a quarter leaves today. The other three quarters drop as blocks onto your next three paydays, and you can see them stack up. On payday, a column taller than your money misses an instalment: a late fee, the plan turns red on your credit report, and you cannot split again until you catch up. After 8 months a bank reads that report for your car loan or your lease renewal, and decides.</p>
<p><strong>What you learn by playing:</strong> pay-later is credit; each plan is small, the stack is not; a missed instalment costs fees and shows on your report; needs and wants compete for the same future paydays; and how much of next month is already spent before it starts.</p>
<p><strong>Real UAE rules, cited in the repository:</strong> the Central Bank of the UAE treats pay-later plans as short-term credit (Finance Companies Regulation C 3/2023): no interest, an affordability check, and all fees on a plan capped at 30% of the price, which the game enforces. Since July 2026, pay-later plans appear on UAE credit reports (Etihad Credit Bureau); under the current model they do not change the credit score, but lenders can see them. Shops, the app and the bank in the game are fictional; prices, income and the AED 50 late fee are illustrative.</p>
<p><strong>UAE edition and accessibility:</strong> English and Arabic (right to left), money in dirhams. Settings on the title screen: large text, high contrast, calm (no offer timer), plain words, sound off; Pause in the top bar, or Esc.</p>
<p>Eight paydays and 24 offers a season, one thumb. Plays on phones and desktops. Made by Design Master Solutions. Code: <a href="https://github.com/DesignMaster-Solutions/stacked-zoud">github.com/DesignMaster-Solutions/stacked-zoud</a>.</p>

## Instructions

<p>Tap or click. Each offer: Pay, Split 4, or Skip. Dashed blocks show where a split would land. Tap "Back to the sales" after each payday. Esc or Pause stops the timer.</p>

## Jam form: Making FinLit Fun (field 48715)

It is sale season in Dubai and every offer has three buttons: pay now, split in 4, or skip. Splitting feels free because only a quarter leaves today, but the other three quarters drop as blocks onto your next three paydays, which stand on screen as columns with your monthly money drawn as a line. You watch the future fill up before you live it. A column taller than your money misses an instalment: a late fee (capped like the UAE rule at 30% of the price for all fees on a plan), the plan turns red on your credit report and new splits pause until you catch up. After 8 months a bank reads the report for your car loan or lease renewal. Needs (car repair, the fridge, school fees) compete with wants for the same paydays, so the best play is not "never split" but "split a need only when the future has room". The end screen shows how much of each payday was already spent before it arrived, the fees, what is still owed, and the real UAE rules with six questions to ask before you split.
