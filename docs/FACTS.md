## 1. CBUAE: BNPL as "Short-Term Credit" under the Finance Companies Regulation

**Status: VERIFIED**

### Primary sources

- **A. Regulation text.** CBUAE Rulebook, "Finance Companies Regulation", **C 3/2023**, page header "Effective from 29/9/2023", "Status: In-Force".
  URL: https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/finance-companies-regulation-0 (also reachable as https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/node/5072; PDF: https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/sites/default/files/en_net_file_store/CBUAE_EN_5072_VER1.pdf)
  The previous regulation (C 112/2018, effective 24/4/2018) is marked "Repealed": "This Regulation has been replaced by the new 'Finance Companies Regulation C 3/2023'." (https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/finance-companies-regulation)
- **B. CBUAE press release, 27 December 2023.** "CBUAE Introduces Framework for the Regulation of Short Term Credit Facilities".
  URL: https://centralbank.ae/media/izlhi5rb/cbuae-introduces-framework-for-the-regulation-of-short-term-credit-facilities_en.pdf
  > "Abu Dhabi (27 December 2023): Acknowledging the global shifts in consumer financial trends and the increasing demand for "Buy-Now, Pay-Later" and similar credit products and services, the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) announces the issuance of the newly amended Finance Companies Regulation."
- **C. CBUAE board approval, 15 June 2023.** "Chaired by Mansour bin Zayed, CBUAE board takes measures to limit impact of interest rate hikes on financial sector".
  URL: https://centralbank.ae/media/jbmbezfh/chaired-by-mansour-bin-zayed-cbuae-board-takes-measures-to-limit-impact-of-interest-rate-hikes-on-financial-sector-en.pdf

Do not state an exact effective day.

### Does the regulation use the words "Buy Now Pay Later"?

No. The regulation text uses only "Short-Term Credit" (zero hits for "Buy Now" or "BNPL" in the rulebook page). The BNPL link is made by the CBUAE in sources B and C.

### Definition (Art. 4, Definitions)

> "Short-Term Credit: any credit which is granted to a Borrower for a period of not more than twelve (12) months, for the express purposes of purchasing specified, identifiable goods or services, without interest being charged nor a lien being placed against collateral nor a security deposit being required from the Borrower;"

> "Fee: Any fees, charges, penalties and commissions incurred on a product and/or service by a Borrower;"

> "Credit Information Agency: Al Etihad Credit Bureau and any credit information agency established for the purpose of credit reporting requirements in the UAE;"

### Rules (Art. 23 Permitted Activities, applies to Restricted Licence Finance Companies and Agents)

- **Credit limit, Art. 23.1:** "The maximum total Short-Term Credit extended to a Borrower by a Restricted Licence Finance Company or Agent, at any given time must not exceed Twenty Thousand Dirham (AED 20,000) or the total of three (3) months' verified net income of the Borrower, whichever is lower (subject to any restrictions established in relevant UAE laws)."
- **Affordability, Art. 23.2:** "The maximum credit granted to a Borrower should be based on the results of affordability assessments conducted, given due consideration to the repayment ability, reducing the risk of over indebtedness and ensuring the fair treatment of Borrowers."
- **No interest, Art. 23.3:** "Restricted Licence Finance Companies or Agents must not charge interest on Short-Term Credit."
- **Fee cap, Art. 23.4:** "The total Fees, including late payment Fees charged on any Short-Term Credit by a Restricted Licence Finance Company or Agent, must not exceed 30% of the original credit amount. Therefore, the maximum recoverable amount from a Borrower on any Short-Term Credit, including where the credit is restructured, rescheduled, rolled-over or where there is any other deviation from the original agreement, must not total more that 130% of the value of the original credit granted to the Borrower. The Central Bank will review this requirement at regular intervals [...]"
- **Duration, Art. 23.5:** "The repayment term of Short-Term Credit offered by a Restricted Licence Finance Company or an Agent must not exceed twelve (12) months from the date of the original agreement to lend, including any form of credit restructuring, rescheduling, roll-over [...]"
- **No early demand, Art. 23.6:** "[...] must honour the original term of the Short-Term Credit and must not demand full payment prior to the end of the term [...]"
- **No security, Art. 23.7:** "Borrowers' assets, including movable and immovable property, must not be used [...] to secure (whether fully or partially), any Short-Term Credit [...]"

Not in the regulation: **no AED cap per late fee, no fixed late-fee amount, no limit on the number of instalments** (the only cap is the 30% total-fee ceiling and the 12-month term). "Pay in 4" is not a regulatory term.

### Credit bureau and affordability (Art. 24 Credit Reports)

- **Art. 24.1:** "Restricted Licence Finance Companies and Agents must strictly adhere to the following credit reporting requirements: a. Provide credit information of Borrowers to the Credit Information Agency, based on the terms and conditions of the Credit Information Agency; and b. Request credit information of Borrowers from the Credit Information Agency, before extending credit to Borrowers in amounts of five thousand Dirham (AED 5,000) or higher."
- **Art. 24.4:** "[...] must disclose to Borrowers that credit information will be retrieved from (where applicable) and shared with the Credit Information Agency; and explain the implications of borrowing, late payment and default, on the Borrower's Credit Report."
- **Art. 24.5:** "[...] must review Borrower information to conduct an affordability assessment to verify the Borrower's solvency and ability to repay [...] The credit information reviewed must include the Borrower's Credit Report in the case of credits granted in amounts of five thousand Dirham (AED 5,000) or more."
- **Art. 24.6:** "[...] must not grant a Short-Term Credit product with a credit limit that is unaffordable for the Borrower based on the results of the affordability assessment."

### Disclosure (Art. 29 Transparency and Disclosures)

- **Art. 29.3:** "Advertising [...] must in all cases, indicate to Borrowers in a clear and conspicuous manner, that products being offered are Short-Term Credit."
- **Art. 29.4:** "[...] must provide comprehensive and transparent information on key product features including, but not limited to, the total amount and expiry date of the credit, the process for accessing and repayment of the credit, related Fees, actions in the event of default, and Borrower rights."
- **Art. 29.6:** "[...] must disclose to Borrowers, the total Fees payable [...] as a total amount/value and as a percentage of the credit value. [...] provide clear examples of how the Fees will be calculated and applied, particularly as they relate to any late payment Fees."
- **Art. 29.7:** "[...] must advise Borrowers of the consequences of late payment and encourage them to make prompt settlement. Any applicable late payment Fees must be explicitly outlined to Borrowers, and must be reasonable."
- **Art. 29.9:** "[...] must clearly disclose the available provisions for hardship assistance to Borrowers who experience financial difficulties [...]"

### Scope note

Art. 3 excludes "Any credit activity, whereby the payment to the vendor directly by the purchaser of a good or service is completed on an instalment basis and not conducted by way of business" (a shop's own private instalment plan not run as a business).

## 2. Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB): BNPL data on credit reports

**Status: VERIFIED** (bureau's own press release and own FAQ)

Note: the bureau now brands itself "Etihad Credit Bureau" on **etihadbureau.ae** (aecb.gov.ae did not resolve from here on 2026-11-04). Legal name in the site footer: "AlEtihad Credit Bureau P.J.S.C."

### Press release, 27 July 2026

- Title: "Etihad Credit Bureau Expands UAE Credit Reporting Ecosystem with Buy Now Pay Later Data"; subtitle "Tabby and Tamara account information to be reflected on credit reports from July 2026".
- URL: https://etihadbureau.ae/about/newsarticlesearchpage/newsarticles/2026/bnpllaunch
- Date as printed: "Abu Dhabi, UAE, 27 July 2026" (a search snippet showed a "Related News" list on another ECB page dated 29 Jul 2026; use 27 July 2026, the date printed on the release).

> "Etihad Credit Bureau, the UAE's federal entity for credit information and data-driven insights, has expanded the scope of information available within UAE credit reports through the inclusion of Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) account information from leading providers Tabby and Tamara, effective July 2026."

> "The addition of BNPL account information will apply to both existing and new customers of Tabby and Tamara in the UAE and will include relevant historical transactions. This enhancement marks an important step towards providing a more comprehensive view of consumers' financial obligations, enabling lenders and authorised entities to conduct more robust risk assessments."

The press release does **not** mention the credit score.

### Effect on the score: bureau FAQ

- URL: https://etihadbureau.ae/Support/FAQs (section "Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)", item path /en/Support/FAQs/Personal/PersonalSubCategories/BNPL/howdoesbnplreportingaffectmycreditscore). Read 2026-11-04; no date on the FAQ.

> Q: "How does BNPL reporting affect my Credit Score?"
> A: "Under the current Credit Score model, BNPL reporting does not affect your Credit Score. However, BNPL information will still appear on your Credit Report and may be considered by organizations that review your report when making decisions."

> Q: "What Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) information appears on my Credit Report?"
> A: "The following BNPL information may appear on your credit report: Number of contracts (active and closed) Outstanding balance Contract status Remaining installments"

> Q: "How often is BNPL information updated?" A: "BNPL information is updated frequently by the information provider."

> Q: "The information reported on my BNPL is not accurate. How can I correct it?" A: "[...] you can submit a Data Correction Request through the Etihad Credit Bureau app. [...] Data correction requests are typically reviewed by the information provider within 7 to 10 working days."

Game implication: the game may say BNPL shows on the **credit report** and that a bank **may consider it**; it must **not** say BNPL lowers the **AECB credit score** under the current model. Secondary claims that a missed BNPL payment "counts twice" or hurts the score (uaefintechvibes.com, digiflow.ae) contradict the bureau and must not be used.

## 4. Official consumer guidance: questions to ask before using BNPL

**Status: NOT FOUND (as a ready-made list)**

No CBUAE, Etihad Credit Bureau or other UAE official page with a "questions to ask before using BNPL" list was found. Lists that do exist are foreign (US FTC, CFPB, NYC DCA) and are not UAE sources.

Usable primary substitute: the information the regulation obliges providers to give (Art. 24.4, 29.4, 29.6, 29.7, 29.9, quoted in section 1). A paraphrase that stays inside those articles:

1. What is the total amount and when is the last payment? (Art. 29.4)
2. What are all the fees, as a total and as a % of the purchase? (Art. 29.6)
3. How is a late fee calculated, with an example? (Art. 29.6, 29.7)
4. What happens if I miss a payment or default? (Art. 29.4, 29.7)
5. Will this be shared with the credit bureau and how can late payment show on my report? (Art. 24.4)
6. What help is there if I have financial difficulty? (Art. 29.9)

Attribute these as "based on what UAE rules require providers to tell you (CBUAE Finance Companies Regulation C 3/2023)", not as an official CBUAE checklist.
