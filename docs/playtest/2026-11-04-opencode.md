## Top 3 Priorities to Make It MORE FUN (within one week)

1. **Show the owed limit before Split locks.** Add a small "owed" chip on the HUD that turns amber/red as `owed` approaches 7000 AED, and show the remaining headroom on the Split button tooltip. Right now the player discovers Split is disabled only after pressing it, with no explanation why. The `why_limit` string already exists (`why_limit`) but is only shown when the button is disabled — move it to a proactive warning so the player can plan around the limit instead of hitting a wall.

2. **Make Split available after a plan goes red, or give a clear "fix late plan" path.** Currently one late instalment freezes Split globally (`why_paused` blocks Split until all red plans clear). A first-time player who misses one instalment is locked out of the core mechanic for the rest of the season. Add a "catch up" pay option on red plans (pay the full remaining balance now) so the player can unblock Split and keep playing, rather than watching every future offer's Split button stay grey.

3. **Cap wants at prices the player can actually split in the second half.** In Season A, months 4-8 almost every want above AED 800 had Split disabled (limit reached) and Pay Now disabled (cash too low), leaving Skip as the only action on ~10 offers. Either lower want prices later in the season, or refund a portion of owed when a plan closes, so the "fun/buying" path can actually buy things past month 3. Without this, trying to have fun is a dead end after the first quarter of the season.
