# Playtest Report: Stacked (Zoud GameLab)

**Date**: 2026-11-04  
**Tester**: Gemini (Antigravity Agent pair-programming session)  
**Report File**: `docs/playtest/2026-11-04-gemini.md`  

---

## 1. Context

* **Build**: Git commit `b79b9e5` (`feat: budget (US$ 47,520, 6 months) with arithmetic test; fix: sign stays next to the number in Arabic`), deployed live at [https://designmaster-solutions.github.io/stacked-zoud/](https://designmaster-solutions.github.io/stacked-zoud/).
* **Device**: Desktop Chrome on Windows MiniPC-i9 (`browse minipc`), dedicated agent window, 1920×1080 viewport.
* **Profile**: First-time player encountering the game blind without examining source code beforehand.
* **Hypothesis Under Test**: *"A first-time player understands that splitting fills future paydays and changes choices because of it, and wants to play a second season."*
* **Summary Verdict on Hypothesis**: **Partially confirmed, but severely impeded by pacing and interface ambiguity.** The core visual metaphor (stacking blocks on future payday columns) successfully conveys debt commitment. However, the aggressive countdown timer (~5–7 seconds per offer) punishes first-time players who pause to read columns or evaluate math, frequently causing accidental timeouts and misclicks on subsequent offers. Furthermore, the penalty mechanism for "skipping a need" (-AED 728 / -AED 908) is disproportionately brutal when triggered accidentally via timer expiration, frustrating player agency rather than encouraging deliberate financial planning.

---

## 2. Journey and Completion Criteria

The playtest executed four journeys:
1. **Season A (`?seed=11`) — High-Spender Persona ("Fun / Buy Things")**:
   - Player prioritized purchasing wants and exploring the "Split in 4" BNPL mechanism.
   - Completed all 8 months (Paydays 1 through 8).
   - Tested stacking limits, wallet depletion, and over-committing future salaries.
2. **Season B (`?seed=12`) — Prudent Saver Persona ("Bank Approval")**:
   - Player prioritized cash payments, zero debt accumulation, and meeting essential needs.
   - Completed all 8 months (Paydays 1 through 8).
   - Tested whether strict avoidance of BNPL reliably delivers bank approval and whether needs can be managed purely via cash.
3. **Arabic Localization Test (`?lang=ar`)**:
   - Tested right-to-left (RTL) layout, Arabic typography, countdown animation direction, and currency sign formatting on the first 2 offers.
4. **Accessibility / Calm Mode Test (`?a11y=calm,large,contrast`)**:
   - Tested untimed ("Calm") gameplay, high-contrast dark theme, enlarged typography, and visual readability of stacked bar charts on the first 2 offers.

**Completion Criteria**: Both 8-month seasons were played to the bank verdict screen; all real numeric values were recorded as rendered; 12 verification screenshots were inspected and archived in `docs/playtest/shots/`.

## 3. Observations

### 3.1 Title Screen
- **What worked**: The bar chart logo with a dotted yellow limit line immediately signals the core theme: exceeding your threshold creates danger. The tagline *"Every 'pay in 4' lands on a payday you have not lived yet"* provides an intuitive mental anchor.
- **Points of confusion**: The relationship between "Good days" and financial success is not explained on the title screen. A player does not know whether "Good days" is a secondary score, a health bar, or a bank approval requirement.
- *Evidence*: `docs/playtest/shots/season-a-01-title.png`

### 3.2 Season A (Seed 11: "Have Fun and Buy Things")
- **Month 1 (Offers 1–3)**:
  - Offer 1: Jacket (AED 650; Pay AED 650, Split 4 × AED 163, Skip). Wallet starts at AED 2,600.
  - While examining the dashed preview blocks (+AED 163 on P2, P3, P4), the shrinking green countdown bar ran out (~6 seconds). The game automatically skipped Offer 1 and advanced to Offer 2.
  - Offer 2: New phone (AED 2,990). "Pay AED 2,990" was disabled with "Not enough in the wallet" (Wallet AED 2,600). Split required 4 × AED 748.
  - Offer 3: Weekend trip (AED 2,690; Split 4 × AED 673). Player clicked Split. 1st instalment (AED 673) left wallet immediately, leaving AED 1,927.
- **Payday 2**:
  - Income added: +AED 1,800. Deducted: Weekend trip instalment -AED 673.
  - Wallet updated to AED 3,054. Good days: 5.
  - Payday chart clearly displayed P3 at 37% (AED 673) and P4 at 37% (AED 671).
  - *Evidence*: `docs/playtest/shots/season-a-03-payday2.png`
- **Month 2**:
  - Offer 1: Sneakers (AED 440; Split 4 × AED 110). Player clicked Split.
  - Offer 2: Big TV (AED 2,220; Split 4 × AED 555).
  - Stacking effect was immediately visible: P3 rose to 68% (AED 1,228), P4 rose to 68% (AED 1,226).
- **Month 3 & Month 4**:
  - Player split Headphones (AED 960; 4 × AED 240) and Smart watch (AED 1,540; 4 × AED 385).
  - In Month 4, Offer 3 presented Sofa (AED 2,550; Split 4 × AED 638). Adding AED 638 to P5 (which already had AED 1,476 scheduled) pushed P5 to AED 2,114 — 117% of monthly salary (AED 1,800).
- **Payday 5 (Overdraft Mechanics)**:
  - Payday 5 income arrived (+AED 1,800).
  - Instalments deducted totaled AED 2,114 (Big TV -555, Car repair -393, Smart watch -385, Concert tickets -143, Sofa -638).
  - *Observed behavior*: Because the player had AED 1,693 left in the wallet, the game absorbed the AED 314 deficit directly from wallet savings, reporting "All instalments paid" and reducing wallet to AED 1,379.
  - *Player confusion*: The title screen stated: *"a column taller than your money misses an instalment: a late fee, and the plan goes red on your credit report."* In reality, the column being taller than monthly salary (1,800) did NOT trigger a late fee because savings covered it. The phrase "taller than your money" conflates monthly salary with total liquid wallet cash.
- **Month 5 & 6 (The Debt Trap)**:
  - Month 5 presented NEED: "The fridge died (AED 1,820; Skipping costs AED 728 next payday)". Wallet had AED 1,379, so Pay Now was disabled.
  - Due to rapid timer countdown, the player attempted to split, but the timer elapsed mid-action. Month 6 Payday penalized the player: "Making do without it -AED 728", plus AED 1,748 in existing instalments. Total deductions on Payday 6 were AED 2,476 against AED 1,800 income!
  - Wallet dropped from AED 1,379 to AED 512, then to AED 329.
  - By Month 6 Offer 2 (New phone AED 3,200), the player could not even afford the 25% down payment (AED 800 > AED 329). Both "Pay" and "Split" buttons displayed "Not enough in the wallet · Not enough in the wallet". The player was locked out of purchasing anything for the remainder of the season.
- **Season A End Screen**:
  - Result: **"APPROVED, WITH CONDITIONS"**
  - Subtext: *"One late instalment, or a lot still owed on plans: a bigger deposit first."*
  - Stats:
    - Average payday already spent before it arrived: **68%**
    - Next month already spent: **67%**
    - Late fees paid: **AED 0**
    - Still owed on plans: **AED 2,726**
    - Things bought: **11**
    - Needs skipped: **1**
    - Left in the wallet: **AED 448**
    - Good days: **20**
  - Credit Report listed 3 active ongoing plans: Jacket (AED 181), School fees (AED 1,090), Game console (AED 1,455), with Weekend trip, Big TV, and Car repair paid off.
  - *Evidence*: `docs/playtest/shots/season-a-04-endscreen.png`

### 3.3 Season B (Seed 12: "Bank Approval")
- **Month 1 & 2 Strategy**:
  - Deliberately skipped expensive wants (Big TV AED 2,520, New phone AED 3,450, Sofa AED 2,690).
  - Wallet grew from AED 2,600 to AED 4,400, then AED 5,592. Debt owed remained strictly AED 0.
- **The Timer Flaw on Needs**:

### 3.5 Accessibility & Calm Mode Test (`?a11y=calm,large,contrast`)
- High Contrast: Pure `#000000` deep black background, bright white container borders, high-visibility yellow numbers, and sharp cyan buttons.
- Calm Mode: **The countdown timer is completely removed.** The offer stays on screen until the player makes a decision. This transforms the game from a frantic reflex test into a thoughtful budgeting puzzle.
- **Visual Clipping Defect**: On low-cost items (e.g. Offer 2: "Dinner out AED 290", Split 4 × AED 73), the vertical bar representing AED 73 out of AED 1,800 is only ~4% of column height (~6–8px). The text label `+AED 73` inside the dashed preview box vertically overflows and collides with the horizontal dashed lines.
- *Evidence*: `docs/playtest/shots/a11y-01-offer1.png`, `docs/playtest/shots/a11y-02-offer2.png`

## 5. Top 3 Priorities to Make It More Fun Within One Week

### Priority 1: Replace the Frantic Countdown Timer with "Tension Pacing" or Turn-Based Offers
* **Problem**: Financial literacy requires arithmetic evaluation: comparing wallet balance, monthly income, and future stacked obligations. The current 5–7 second timer forces frantic snap judgments. First-time players frequently lose offers to timeouts and suffer catastrophic accidental penalties on Needs (BUG1, D1).
* **Fix**:
  1. Default to **untimed turn-based decisions** (similar to current `?a11y=calm`), OR give each offer a generous 15–20 second window with an explicit "Fast-Forward / Next" button for impatient players.
  2. Implement an input debouncer (300ms cooldown) when an offer transitions so that late clicks on an expiring offer do not bleed into the next offer.
  3. Add a visual "Sale Ending Soon!" pulse only in the final 3 seconds.

### Priority 2: Visual "Debt Trap" Warning & Celebration on Payoffs
* **Problem**: When a player stacks multiple splits, the chart simply turns columns cyan/salmon. There is no visceral dramatic moment when debt becomes dangerous, nor any celebration when a 4-month plan is finally completed.
* **Fix**:
  1. **Visual Tension**: When a future column exceeds 75% of income, color the excess bar amber; when it exceeds 100%, render the exceeding block in flashing warning red with an audible stress sound effect or screen shake.
  2. **Celebration on Payoff**: When the 4th instalment of a plan clears on Payday, display a satisfying green "PAID IN FULL" stamp animation with a positive chime, showing debt counter decrease and "Good Days" burst.

### Priority 3: Interactive BNPL Simulator Slider / Hover Preview on Offer Card
* **Problem**: Currently, the player only sees static dashed boxes for the currently viewed offer. They cannot experiment with different financial paths (e.g., "What if I pay 50% now instead of 25%?", or "Can I prepay an old plan to make room for this new one?").
* **Fix**:
  1. Allow hovering / tapping on "Split" to dynamically highlight exactly which future paydays will be burdened and show the projected remaining disposable income for each month.
  2. Provide a "Pay Off Early" button on active plans in the Credit Report sidebar during Payday, allowing players who accumulated savings to clear stacked columns before taking on new offers.
